# Editor Checklist

Use this checklist before approving a draft for human review.

## Assignment Fit

- The draft answers the assigned topic.
- The angle has not drifted beyond the brief.
- The intended audience and format are respected.

## Evidence

- Key factual claims are supported by sources.
- Citations map cleanly to the claims they support.
- Primary sources are used where practical.
- Conflicting sources are acknowledged.
- Uncertainty is visible instead of hidden.

## Risk

- High-risk claims have stronger evidence.
- Recent events include concrete dates.
- Legal, health, financial, and public safety claims are handled cautiously.
- Private individuals are treated with care.
- No unsupported allegations are presented as fact.

## Quality

- The story has a clear structure.
- The lead is accurate and proportionate.
- Context is sufficient for a reader unfamiliar with the topic.
- The draft avoids filler, invented color, and unsupported causal claims.
- The conclusion does not overstate what the reporting proves.

## Decision

- Accept only if deterministic fact checks pass.
- Request revision when evidence, clarity, or context is incomplete.
- Reject when the piece is materially unsupported, unsafe, or outside scope.
